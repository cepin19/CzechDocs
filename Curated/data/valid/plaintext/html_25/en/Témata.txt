What do you need to know about life in the Czech Republic? Browse through useful information to help you navigate the Czech environment with ease and find out whom to contact in different life situations.

Prague

Prague
Find out how authorities in Prague work — whether it’s the Prague City Hall or individual city districts. Their services can (and in many cases must) also be used by foreigners.

Residence in the Czech Republic

Residence in the Czech Republic
Reliable sources and information about institutions you can contact regarding residence permit in the Czech Republic —including contact details and how to book an appointment.

Social Security

Social Security
Overview of the social security system in the Czech Republic, including mandatory social insurance, state social support, and assistance in material need.

Family and Children

Family and Children
Leisure activities for children and families in Prague, information on resolving family issues, and assistance for domestic violence victims.

Education

Education
Outline of the Czech education system and foreign education recognition. Integration opportunities for children with a different mother tongue — tips for tutoring, preparatory courses, and contacts for counseling institutions.

Czech Language Courses

Czech Language Courses
Czech language courses — different levels and forms of teaching, including online or video courses and preparatory courses for the permanent residence exam.

Healthcare

Healthcare
Healthcare services in the Czech Republic: mandatory health insurance, standard medical care, what to do in emergency situations.

Work

Work
Conditions for entering the labor market: practical advice on job searching, how to obtain a trade license, and what are your obligations as an entrepreneur.

Solving Crisis Situations

Solving Crisis Situations
Assistance in difficult life situations, such as homelessness, domestic violence, or labor exploitation — contacts for specialized non-profit organizations.

Transport

Transport
Essential information about transportation in Prague, including public transport tickets, parking zones, and exchanging a driver’s license for a Czech one, etc.

Culture

Culture
Explore an overview of the most significant cultural institutions where foreigners can participate in social life.

Assistance for Foreigners

Assistance for Foreigners
Find out which non-profit organizations can provide free assistance with integrating into Czech society and addressing various life situations.

Volunteering

Volunteering
Get involved as a volunteer and gain new friends, experiences, and a deeper understanding of local culture. Discover different opportunities to make meaningful use of your free time and contribute to positive changes in society.
