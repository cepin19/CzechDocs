There are a number of non-profit organizations in Prague that offer support to foreigners in various aspects of integration and other life situations. Their services are provided free of charge thanks to funding from various sources, including public and private.
Integration Centre Prague o. p. s.
Integration Centre Prague (ICP) is a non-profit organization operating in Prague since 2012, offering free services to migrants from non-EU countries. ICP is part of a network of integration centers located across the Czech Republic.
All ICP services are funded by European funds, the budget of the City of Prague, and other grants, making them free of charge for clients.
Services Offered:
Social counseling
Assistance in finding nursery, primary, and secondary schools and communicating with them
Recognition of foreign education (nostrification)
Assistance in finding a doctor and information on health insurance
Advice on marriage, birth, or death registration
Help with job search, filling out forms, and writing a CV
Basic advice on pensions and social benefits
Information on citizenship application
Legal counseling
Extending residence permit, changing the purpose of stay, applying for residence, appeals
Family reunification
Reviewing documents and help with understanding official documents, primarily those from the Czech Ministry of the Interior
Basic consultations on contracts (rental, employment, etc.)
Basic advice on marriage, discrimination issues, document verification, etc.
Accompaniment to institutions
Assistance in communicating with authorities
Interpreting and assistance during meetings
Contact Point for Foreigners – ICP
Assistance and interpreting services within the premises of the Prague City Hall (MHMP)
Accompaniment and interpreting services outside the City Hall if necessary (e.g., to schools, healthcare facilities, or other authorities)
Providing basic consultations
You can find more information here
Czech language courses
Various levels
Courses for both adults and children
Educational seminars
Expert lectures on Czech history, traditions, residency in the Czech Republic, healthcare, social, and education systems
Cultural and community activities
Events aimed at bringing Czechs and migrants together and fostering friendly coexistence
