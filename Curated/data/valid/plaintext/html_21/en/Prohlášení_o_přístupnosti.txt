Accessibility Statement
The Integration Centre Prague, o.p.s., is committed to making its website accessible in compliance with Act No. 99/2019 Coll., on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications and on amendments to Act No. 365/2000 Coll., on public administration information systems and on amendments to some other acts, as amended, which implements Directive (EU) 2016/2102 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 October 2016 on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications.This accessibility statement applies to the website newinprague.cz
Compliance Status
This website is fully compliant with Act No. 99/2019 Coll., on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications and on amendments to Act No. 365/2000 Coll., on public administration information systems and on amendments to some other acts, as amended. It meets the “AA” level of Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1.
The accessibility statement was prepared on 7 October 2024.
Supported Web Browsers
Optimal display and functionality are supported by the following web browsers:For desktop: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, SafariFor mobile devices: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari
Feedback and Contact Information
We are continually working to ensure that this website is fully accessible to all users. If you encounter any problems, please let us know. To share suggestions, observations, or information about any display issues with this website, please contact us by email at info@newinprague.cz.
Enforcement Procedures
If you receive an unsatisfactory response to a notification or request submitted under Article 7(1)(b) of the Directive on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications, please contact the relevant enforcement authority:
