Engaging in cultural and social life is one of the key aspects of integration. Prague offers a wide network of cultural venues, such as theaters, clubs, music halls, and dance venues.
The Prague City Gallery provides not only permanent exhibits and exhibitions but also accompanying programs.
The National Museum is the largest museum in the Czech Republic, consisting of five institutions: the Natural Science Museum, the Historical Museum, the Czech Museum of Music, the Náprstek Museum of Asian, African, and American Cultures, and the National Museum Library.
The National Gallery Prague manages the largest collection of fine art in the Czech Republic, offering various events and educational programs for children and adults.
The Prague City Museum offers educational programs, including lectures, guided tours, and workshops for visitors.
Museum Kampa features a significant collection of modern and contemporary art.
The National Museum of Agriculture is a great place for the whole family, with a number of interactive exhibits to keep both children and adults entertained. 
The National Technical Museum offers extensive exhibitions focused on the history and development of technology, covering transportation, astronomy, photography, architecture, and other scientific and technical fields. There are even technical afterschool clubs for children.
At the Municipal Library of Prague you can borrow not only books in Czech and other languages but also e-readers, music, movies, and art. It also hosts cultural events, children’s programs, lectures, workshops, concerts, and theater performances. Free internet access is available at all library branches.
Don’t know where to go today? Check out the What to do in Prague or Kudy z nudy.cz websites for ideas.
